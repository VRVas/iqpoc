Can I keep my electronic cigarette (e-cigarette) in my checked-in baggage?

## 🤖 Assistant

**No, you cannot keep your electronic cigarette (e-cigarette) in your checked-in baggage with Qatar Airways.**  
E-cigarettes are considered dangerous goods and their carriage as checked baggage is strictly regulated. International Dangerous Goods Regulations require that such items obtain **prior airline approval** to be carried in checked baggage or cargo.  
- **You must contact Qatar Airways or Qatar Airways Cargo offices at least 96 hours before departure** to request approval for carrying an electronic cigarette in checked baggage.
- Failure to obtain approval may result in the item being denied carriage.
- E-cigarettes also cannot be operated at any time on board Qatar Airways flights.

**Summary:**  
- Do NOT place e-cigarettes in checked baggage unless you have received prior approval from Qatar Airways.
- Default policy: Keep e-cigarettes in cabin (hand) baggage and follow battery safety rules.

## 👤 User

Can I carry portable oxygen generator?

## 🤖 Assistant

**Yes, you can carry a portable oxygen generator (specifically a portable oxygen concentrator/POC) on Qatar Airways flights, but you MUST obtain prior approval.**

**Policy details:**
- Portable oxygen generators/concentrators, CPAP machines, ventilators, and respirators are allowed if approved in advance.
- You must submit a Medical Information Form (MEDIF) between **7 days and 48 hours before departure** and present a medical certificate at check-in.
- The device must have a manufacturer’s label showing compliance with QCAA (Qatar Civil Aviation Authority) or FAA requirements.
- Battery-powered devices: Acceptability will depend on battery watt-hour rating or lithium content.
- Devices must be stowed per cabin safety rules.

**Summary Table:**

| Device Type          | Carry-On Allowed | Checked Allowed | Approval Needed | Key Steps                         |
|----------------------|------------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------|
| Portable Oxygen Generator/Concentrator | Yes              | No*             | Yes            | MEDIF, medical certificate, compliance label |

\* Portable oxygen generators are to be carried in the cabin (not checked) and may only be used subject to approval and stowage safety.
